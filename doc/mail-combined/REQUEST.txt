Provenance / license: the PCM sources are pure sine tones I synthesized
myself (48 kHz s16; stereo 440/880 Hz, 5.1 SMPTE order 440/560/680/90/
320/500 Hz), encoded with our own cleanroom AC-4 encoder (c-ac4enc, an
independent implementation; no Dolby SDK, code, or bytes involved).
The two further `.ac4` clips (stereo51, impulse) are likewise tones I
synthesized myself and encoded with c-ac4enc; `garbage.ac4` is 1024
bytes from a seeded PRNG (seed 0xAC40), not audio at all; the seek
`.pcm` is a decoded slice of my own clip. No third-party content in
those eleven. I release all eleven files for unrestricted
redistribution as part of the FFmpeg FATE suite.
